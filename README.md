## Star//Runner — Neon Drift

A complete, arcade-style space survival game. Open `index.html` in a browser (or serve this folder locally) and launch your mission. Steer with **← / →** or **A / D**; on touchscreens, drag left and right across the playfield. Collect cyan energy to charge a one-hit shield, dodge the pink asteroids, and beat your best distance. Press **P** or **Esc** to pause. Your best score is saved in this browser.
